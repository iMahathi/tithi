1. Information that stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device. We never receive it:

  • Your settings: language, city, rashi, theme, notification choices and similar preferences.
  • Your own entries: birth details for the kundli (name, date, time and place of birth), notes, japa counts, Likhita japam, the puja reminder time, and anything you type into the everyday tools. These tools include the calorie, water and pregnancy-date calculators, the money and tax calculators, the letter writer and the résumé maker.
  • Astrology results: kundli charts, kundli matching, doshas and panchangam timings are calculated on your phone.
  • Saved content: copies of downloaded lists and pictures, kept so the app works faster and offline. You can remove them in Settings › Clear cached data.

The compass uses your phone’s motion sensors. That reading stays on the phone. Greeting cards are saved to your Pictures/Tithi folder only when you choose to save one, and are shared only when you choose to share one.

If backup is turned on in your phone’s settings, Android may include the app’s data in your device backup to your Google account. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy.

2. Information sent over the internet

Some features fetch content from the internet. When they do, the server that answers sees your device’s IP address, as any website would. This is what each feature sends:

Videos, live darshan and bhakti audio

Sent to
YouTube (Google)
What is sent
A request for the video or audio you chose. Thumbnails are loaded from YouTube’s image servers. If you turn off Settings › Playback, videos and songs open in the YouTube app instead.

Live TV and radio

Sent to
The servers of the broadcaster or streaming service that carries the channel
What is sent
A request for the stream you chose. Nothing about you is added.

The “More services” screens

Sent to
The content server at app.ibharat.io. These screens include the older horoscope, devotional, TV, radio, cricket, jobs and gold-rate screens.
What is sent
The city, language, rashi or date you picked on that screen. For cricket predictions, the random install ID is also sent, so that your own predictions can be shown back to you.

Gold and silver spot price

Sent to
Public exchange-rate files at currency-api.pages.dev and cdn.jsdelivr.net
What is sent
Nothing about you. The app just downloads a file.

Pictures and links in the content

Sent to
The websites that host those pictures, or that a link opens in your browser
What is sent
A request for that picture or page. Nothing about you is added.

Jyotishi questions (AI)

When
Only if you type your own question to the AI Jyotishi, in versions of the app where this feature is turned on. The quick readings don’t use the internet.
Sent to
Our AI service, which uses Anthropic’s Claude model to write the answer
What is sent
Your question, the conversation so far, your chosen language, the random install ID, and the calculated chart (planet positions, dasha and similar details). Your name, birth date and birth place are not sent.

3. The random install ID

When you first open Tithi, the app creates a random code and stores it on your phone. It is not linked to your name, phone number, Google account or advertising ID, and we don’t use the advertising ID at all.

It is used for only two things: showing your cricket predictions back to you, and limiting how many AI Jyotishi questions one phone can ask each day. Clearing the app’s storage or reinstalling the app creates a new one.

4. What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.
  • We don’t sell or rent your information, and we don’t share it for advertising or marketing.
  • We don’t access your location. You choose your city yourself.
  • We don’t access your contacts, camera, microphone, call log, SMS or photos.
  • We don’t have user accounts, so we hold no profile of you.

6. Other companies’ services

When a feature uses another company’s service, that company handles the request under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • YouTube: Tithi uses YouTube API Services to play videos. By watching them you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.
  • Anthropic (AI Jyotishi answers): the Anthropic Privacy Policy applies.
  • Google Play and Android (installing, updating and backing up the app): the Google Privacy Policy applies.
  • The content server, broadcasters and websites listed in section 2 apply their own policies to the requests they receive.

7. Keeping and deleting data

  • On your phone: your data stays until you delete it. To remove all of it, go to Android Settings › Apps › Tithi › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  • AI Jyotishi: our AI service does not store your questions or the answers. It keeps only a count of how many questions an install ID has asked today, and deletes that count automatically within about 30 hours.
  • Other services: data held by the services in section 6 is kept according to their own policies.

We hold no information that identifies you. Clearing the app’s storage replaces the install ID, which ends any link to data held under the old one. For any question about your data, email hello.imahathi@gmail.com.

8. Children

Tithi is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

The app downloads content over encrypted HTTPS wherever the source offers it. Some radio and TV broadcasters offer their streams only over plain HTTP. The app sends no personal information with those requests. Your entries on the phone are protected by Android’s app storage, which other apps cannot read.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.

11. Contact

For questions or requests about this policy or your data, email hello.imahathi@gmail.com.
